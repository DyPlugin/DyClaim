# DyClaim 1.2.1

Owners can now let visitors open chests and other storage blocks, while building stays protected. Existing commands, settings and claim data are kept.

## Added

- **Visitor containers.** `/claim containers` lets visitors open chests, trapped chests, barrels, shulker boxes, furnaces, blast furnaces, smokers, hoppers, dispensers, droppers and brewing stands in your claim. Visitors still cannot break or place blocks, and other blocks such as anvils, beds, jukeboxes and lecterns stay protected. Hoppers still cannot pull items across a claim border.
- **Server control.** The new `protection.visitor-containers` setting works like `visitor-doors` and `visitor-trapdoors`: `default` (off), `forced-value` to lock it for the whole server, `player-toggle` to stop owners from changing it, and per-world overrides under `claim-rules.worlds`. `/claim info` shows the current state.

## Upgrading

Stop the server, back up `plugins/DyClaim`, replace the old JAR with `DyClaim-1.2.1.jar` and start the server. The new setting is added with its default (off), so nothing changes until you turn it on. Claim data and translations are kept, and the new messages are added in every language.
